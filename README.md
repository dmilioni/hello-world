## Duck Crossing

A Frogger-style arcade game made for phones: [`duck-crossing/index.html`](duck-crossing/index.html).

Guide your duck from the dock to its nest. Swim through the boat channel without getting hit,
then ride logs, turtles and other floating things across the rapids. Fill all five nests to move
on to the next level.

| Level | Water | What's new |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Morning Lake | Tugboats, speedboats, canoes and sailboats; logs and turtles |
| 2 | Sunset Harbor | Long ferries, faster speedboats, more diving turtles |
| 3 | Night Marsh | Dark water, patrol boats with searchlights, crocodiles that bite, sinking lily pads |
| 4 | Frozen Fjord | Icebreakers, ice floes that crack and sink, snow |
| 5 | Storm Bay | Wind gusts that push the duck sideways, rain and lightning |

After level 5 the waters repeat, faster each time. You can start at any level from the title screen.

- Swipe on the board or use the on-screen pad to hop. Tapping the board hops forward.
- Keyboard: arrow keys or WASD, P to pause.
- Points: 10 per hop forward, 50 per nest plus 10 per second left, 200 for a lost duckling,
  1000 for filling all five nests. You get an extra duck every 5000 points.

It is a single HTML file with no build step. Open it in any mobile browser, or enable GitHub Pages
for this repo and visit `/duck-crossing/` on your phone.
